Privacy Policy — Shutoko Revival Project Bot

Last updated: 11 October 2026

1. Purpose

The OCR Moderation Bot (“the Bot”) operates exclusively in the Shutoko Revival Project Discord server. It scans images for text associated with common scams, including cryptocurrency scams and fraudulent casino promotions, to help protect the community.

2. Data Processing

The Bot may temporarily process posted images, text extracted through optical character recognition (OCR), and the Discord user ID associated with the post.

All OCR processing is performed locally. Images and extracted text are deleted immediately after processing and are not retained or shared with external OCR services.

3. Legal Basis

Processing is intended to rely on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, specifically preventing scams and maintaining server safety.

4. Automated Moderation

When prohibited content is detected, the Bot automatically kicks the user who posted it. Detection may be inaccurate. Users may contact the server's moderation team to challenge a moderation action.

5. Data Sharing and Retention

The Bot does not sell personal data or use it for advertising. Images and extracted text are not retained. Discord and any relevant infrastructure providers may independently process data under their own privacy policies.

6. Your Rights

Where applicable under the GDPR, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, request restriction of processing, or object to processing. You may also lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, data protection requests, or moderation appeals, contact the Shutoko Revival Project moderation team through the server's designated support channel.

8. Updates

This policy may be updated if the Bot's functionality or data processing practices change.
